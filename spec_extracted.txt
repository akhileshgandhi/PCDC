4. PREVENTING BLIND AI COPY-PASTE
This is the biggest challenge.
The solution is architectural.
Layer 1: Thought Submission
Before AI access is unlocked:
Student must submit:
Initial Analysis
Questions:
What do you think is happening?
What are the possible causes?
What assumptions are you making?
What information is missing?
What would be your tentative solution?
Minimum 200-300 words.
Only after submission:
AI becomes available.
This creates:
Think First → AI Later
Layer 2: AI Interaction Logging
Every interaction stored.
System captures:
Prompts used
AI responses
Time spent
Iterations
Mentor can see:
Student Thinking Path.
Layer 3: Reflection Requirement
After AI interaction:
Student must answer:
What new insights did AI provide?
What did you disagree with?
What changes did you make?
What remains uncertain?
This develops judgment.
Layer 4: Defense Stage
AI asks:
Why is your solution superior?
What are the risks?
What alternative exists?
How would a competitor respond?
This forces reasoning.
Layer 5: Oral Challenge
Randomly selected students:
AI interviews them.
Example:
Why did you recommend this strategy?
If they cannot explain:
Score reduced.
This eliminates copy-paste behavior.
